01 Surety bonds

Bonds on the obligee's own form.

Municipalities, provinces and states publish their own bond forms and expect them back unchanged. necodoc fills the form they published.

How
Upload the obligee's PDF, place or import fields, publish. Each signer (principal, surety, attorney-in-fact) gets a box and a place in the routing order.
Signing
Every generation carries a signing package for DocuSign, Dropbox Sign or Acrobat Sign. Your provider runs the signature.
Limit
Pure XFA forms and forms that need a password to open aren't supported.
ProofPDF overlay templatesSigning packages

Example: Oregon CCB commercial surety bond, fictional principal

02 Certificates of insurance

Certificates from the broker portal or the policy system.

A designed template with typed fields. The same fields drive the guided form a person fills and the JSON your systems post.

How
Fields with types, formats (CAD, dates) and conditions; a JSON Schema per published version; 422 with every field error when data is wrong.
Who
Brokers fill it through an expiring share link; systems call /api/v1 with a scoped key.
Output
PDF/A-2 for your archive, PDF/UA-1 for accessible formats, PAdES certification so a recipient can tell an edited copy.
ProofFields and guided formShare links

Example: certificate of surety bond, Ontario, fictional surety

03 Policy packets

Declarations, wordings and endorsements as one PDF.

Generate each part from its own template, or pull in a static wording, and combine them in the order you set.

How
POST /combine with generations and uploaded PDFs, or a batch delivered as one packet.
Limit
No bookmarks, table of contents or page renumbering across the packet yet.
ProofCombining PDFs into packets

Example: commercial property packet

04 Bulk renewals

The month-end renewal run, behind the day's work.

A batch of up to 10,000 rows from JSON or CSV goes on the queue. Interactive renders jump ahead of it, so a broker issuing a certificate isn't kept waiting.

How
Progress, cancel, invalid rows fail alone; download one ZIP or one combined packet.
Scale
Add workers for the run and remove them after. No licence per worker.
Measured

05 Claims letters

The wording an adjuster sends is the wording legal approved.

Conditional sections and shared clauses mean one reviewed template covers the cases, instead of a folder of near-copies.

How
"Show only if" conditions on sections, repeating rows, snippets for shared clauses, themes for house style.
Control
Clause changes go through review, with test cases and a visual diff before publish.
Limit
PDF only: no Word output, and no email or print channels.
ProofSnippet libraryReview and approval

Example: a rider section shown only when ticked

06 Bilingual output

French first in Québec, English elsewhere, from one template.

Since 2023, contracts of adhesion in Québec must be offered in French first. Keeping two templates means two copies that drift. necodoc keeps one.

How
One template with translations per language, fr-CA included; the request picks the language. Dates, numbers and currency format per locale.
Accessible
PDF/UA-1 output carries the document language for screen readers.
Yours
Translation quality and legal review stay with your forms team.
ProofDocument localization

Example: one certificate template, two languages · FR copy pending review by a fluent speaker
