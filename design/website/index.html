01 Overlay

Fill the forms you already have.

Upload the obligee's bond form or the regulator's PDF and place fields on it. The output is the original page, with your data on it.

  • Flat PDFs with no fields, or import the AcroForm fields that are there
  • AI proposes the boxes; a person accepts each one. Off unless you add a key
  • Government PDFs with an empty password and hybrid XFA forms open as-is
Limit: pure XFA forms and PDFs that need a password to open aren't supported.

02 Speed and scale-out

Fast, and you add workers, not licences.

Renders run as queued jobs on worker processes. Month-end is a replica count, and every render records where its time went.

  • Interactive renders jump ahead of batch rows
  • Batches of up to 10,000 rows from JSON or CSV, with progress, cancel, a ZIP or one packet
  • Every PDF logged with input, version and a timing breakdown, and re-runnable
Limit: throughput is bounded by Postgres, which also carries the queue. We don't claim unlimited workers.
ProofRender queue and scalingBenchmark

03 Insurance fit

Built around bonds, certificates and policy packets.

The vocabulary is yours: principal, surety, obligee, attorney-in-fact, declarations page, renewal run.

  • Signing packages with principal, surety and attorney-in-fact signers
  • Certificates and policy documents combined into one packet
  • English and fr-CA from one template, so the two copies can't drift
  • PDF/A-2 for archives, PDF/UA-1 for accessible formats, PAdES certification
E-signature ready, not e-signature: necodoc prepares the package; your DocuSign, Dropbox Sign or Acrobat Sign account runs the binding signature.

Identity

Does it use our IdP?

Entra ID, Auth0 or SiteMinder over OIDC, SAML 2.0 or a trusted header, with IdP groups mapped to roles. Systems use scoped, expiring, IP-restricted keys.

Single sign-on →

Data residency

Where does policyholder data go?

Your Postgres and your bucket, blob container or disk, in your region. With AI off, which is the default, nothing leaves.

Data flows →

Change control

Who changed the bond wording?

Published versions are immutable. Review is its own permission. The audit log is append-only and keeps before and after diffs.

Audit and review →

Scale and availability

What happens at month-end?

Stateless API and workers scale out. Jobs are queued with priorities; a job on a killed worker retries on another.

Where necodoc stops

What it doesn't do, so you don't find out in month three.

If you need print, email and SMS channels or Word-native authoring, an enterprise CCM suite is the better fit. Compare

No packaged Guidewire or Duck Creek connector.

Your core system calls a versioned REST API with OpenAPI and a JSON Schema per template. The Architecture page shows the pattern.

No vendor SOC 2 or ISO 27001 report.

There is no hosted service to attest. necodoc runs inside your SOC 2 and B-13 boundary and inherits your controls.

PDF only.

No Word or DOCX output, and no print, email or SMS channels.

No ISO or AAIS bureau form library.

You upload or design your own forms.

Not an e-signature service.

It prepares signing packages; your provider runs the legally binding signing.
