Security and governance

Controls you can check, inside a boundary you already audit.

necodoc runs in your account, signs people in through your IdP, and writes its records to your Postgres. This page lists the controls it provides, how each works, and where each one stops.

The one thing that can leave your environment is AI, and it's off by default. If an admin sets ANTHROPIC_KEY, the template assistant sends the draft template (fields, content, sample data) and AI field detection sends page images of the uploaded form to Anthropic's API. Generation data, the policyholder data that fills real documents, isn't sent. Each AI request is in the audit log. Without the key, no request leaves your boundary except webhooks you configure. Audit and logging

Who changed the bond wording, who approved it, and when.

The audit log records the change and its diff. The generation log records every PDF that came out of it. Together they answer the auditor's question without a spreadsheet.

Append-only in the database.

A trigger rejects updates and deletes on audit rows, so the app, an API key or a bug can't rewrite history.

Tamper-resistant, not tamper-evident.

There is no hash chain, and a DBA can disable the trigger. Ship the log to your SIEM if you need proof against your own administrators.

Change control

The business owns the wording. IT owns the data contract.

Editors change content. Fields are the contract your systems code against, published as a JSON Schema per version. Review and publish are separate permissions.

  • Immutable published versions; the API calls a version, not "whatever's current"
  • A reviewer sees structure, page and test changes, then approves or asks for changes
  • Test cases and a visual diff run on the draft before it's published
  • Shared clauses live in snippets, so a wording change is made once
Limit: one review step. Multi-step approval chains aren't supported.

Output integrity

Archive, accessible, certified

PDF/A-2b or 2u for retention, tagged PDF/UA-1 for screen readers, and PAdES certification a recipient can check against an edited copy. Never applied to a file that can't honestly carry it.

What we don't claim

No badges we haven't earned.

There is no hosted service, so there is nothing for an auditor to attest. necodoc runs inside your SOC 2 and B-13 boundary and inherits your controls.

No SOC 2, ISO 27001 or "certified secure".

We describe controls, not attestations.

Not "tamper-proof".

The audit log is tamper-resistant; see above.

Not an e-signature service.

No ESIGN, eIDAS or UECA claims. Your provider signs.

Not "data never leaves" without the AI caveat.

It's true with AI off, the default, and stated at the top of this page.
